# Status

**Status:** active, 2026-11-01

Calendrical is built on Localize 1.5, CLDR 49 and Astro 2.10; 2.2.0 is on hex (2026-10-27) and 2.3.0 is prepared on `main`. 2.3.0 takes all of its astronomy from Astro: the lunisolar, Persian, observational Islamic and astronomical Ecclesiastical calendars answer for dates far from the present, and a day that starts at sunset starts at that local evening's sunset.

Decided 2026-09-28, with Localize: Calendrical moved to CLDR 49 in one significant release, 2.0.0, rather than two a fortnight apart; the CHANGELOG and the [migration guide](guides/migration.md) list its changes.
