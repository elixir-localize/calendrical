# Status

**Status:** active, 2026-11-01

Calendrical is built on Localize 1.6, CLDR 49 and Astro 2.10 (locked at 2.11); 2.5.0 is the current release (2026-11-01). It adds the Bangla calendar of Bangladesh, its 1987 and 2019 revisions and their composite, named from CLDR's Indian calendar with an era of its own, for Tempo's `bengali-revised` date rules and tempo_holidays' Bangladeshi holidays.

Decided 2026-09-28, with Localize: Calendrical moved to CLDR 49 in one significant release, 2.0.0, rather than two a fortnight apart; the CHANGELOG and the [migration guide](guides/migration.md) list its changes.
