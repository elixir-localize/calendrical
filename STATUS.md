# Status

**Status:** active, 2026-10-27

Calendrical is built on Localize 1.4, CLDR 49 and Astro 2.9; 2.1.0 is on hex (2026-10-26) and 2.2.0 is prepared on `main`, the only branch. 2.2.0 adds the sexagenary cycle and year of every lunisolar calendar (`cycle_and_year/1`, `elapsed_years/2`), which Tempo's date rules read a Chinese year by.

Decided 2026-09-28, with Localize: Calendrical moved to CLDR 49 in one significant release, 2.0.0, rather than two a fortnight apart; the CHANGELOG and the [migration guide](guides/migration.md) list its changes.
